INTERPRETATION
============================================================
A p-value below 0.05 indicates evidence of a difference in paired classification outcomes under the exact McNemar test. It does not prove that one model will outperform in the future.

Bootstrap intervals quantify uncertainty around historical accuracy. Overlapping intervals should not be interpreted as a formal equivalence test.

Regime robustness is descriptive and should not be interpreted as proof of profitability.
