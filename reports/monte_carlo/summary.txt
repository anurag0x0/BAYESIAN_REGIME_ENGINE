METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Returns are sampled from observed historical returns.
2. Regime-specific simulations use empirical regime-conditioned returns.
3. No future returns are used to generate the simulated paths.
4. VaR is an empirical lower-tail loss quantile.
5. CVaR is the average simulated loss beyond the VaR threshold.
6. Historical tail scenarios are based on observed return quantiles.
7. Monte Carlo simulation is a risk-analysis framework and does not guarantee future market outcomes.
